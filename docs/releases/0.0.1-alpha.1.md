**Koan's first release.** Koan is a calm, keyboard-first browser for macOS. It brings Zen's way of working to Chromium, and it adds AI agents that work in tabs of their own, beside you, in your own browser.

> [!WARNING]
> **This is an alpha.** It's for early testers. Expect rough edges, and back up anything you can't lose.
> - Settings and profile formats may change between alpha versions.
> - Security fixes from Chromium are applied on a best-effort basis, not on Chrome's schedule.
> - There's no automatic updater yet: install new versions by hand from this page.
> - The known issues are listed below.

### Installing

1. Open the DMG and drag **Koan** to **Applications**.
2. Koan isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS blocks it the first time it opens: *"Apple could not verify 'Koan' is free of malware"*. Allow it once, either way:
   - **Terminal:** `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Koan.app`
   - **Or:** click **Done**, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, and click **Open Anyway** next to the message about Koan.
3. On first launch, macOS asks for your login password so that Koan can create its **"Koan Safe Storage"** key in your keychain. Koan uses that key to encrypt saved passwords and cookies. Click **Always Allow**. Expect this prompt once after each update until Koan is signed with a Developer ID.

## What's in 0.0.1

### Browsing, the Zen way
- **Sidebar tabs and Spaces.** Vertical tabs, pinned tabs and folders. Spaces each have their own icon and colour, and you switch between them from the keyboard.
- **Single toolbar.** Back, forward and reload live in the sidebar. The address bar floats over the page while you type (⌘T, or ⌘L and start typing).
- **Compact mode.** Hide the sidebar and toolbar, and reveal them from the window edge.
- **Glance.** Preview a link in a card over the page (⌥-click), then keep it or close it.
- **Split view.** Two pages side by side or stacked.
- **Library.** History, downloads and recently closed tabs in one place.
- **A shadcn look** in light and dark, with Zen's look available as an option. Onboarding imports your data from other browsers.
- **Chromium 158 underneath.** Chrome's rendering, extensions and DevTools. Every Koan feature can be switched off in `chrome://flags`.

### Fast
This is the first Koan built with Chromium's full release optimisations (ThinLTO and profile-guided optimisation).

### Agents in your browser
Koan lets AI agents (Claude Code, Cursor and others) use your browser as you do, in tabs of their own, without taking over your screen.
- **Set Up Agents** in Koan installs Koan's guide into the agents on your Mac. Agents drive Koan through the `koan` command, which ships inside the app. You approve each agent once.
- **Agents work in their own Space.** Their tabs keep working in the background while you use the rest of Koan. A pill on the tab the agent controls, and a chip in the sidebar, show what it's doing.
- **Take over at any time.** Click into an agent's tab and the agent pauses. Leave the tab for about 5 seconds and it carries on where you left off, and it knows what you changed.
- **Your logins and autofill, without your secrets.** Agents can sign in with your saved logins and fill your saved addresses and cards using Chrome's own autofill. They see the labels but never the values: passwords and card numbers stay hidden from the agent, even in screenshots.
- **You stay in charge.** Agents stop and hand the tab to you for passwords you haven't saved, two-factor codes, CAPTCHAs and the final Pay button.
- **Cheap for agents to use.** Compact page outlines, small screenshots (a small one costs about 5% of a full screenshot's tokens), and several actions in a single call.

## Known issues
- Idle CPU with many tabs open is higher than it should be (about 10% with 8 tabs).
- [#418](https://github.com/Chaitanya045/koan/issues/418): Library → History doesn't scroll.
- [#419](https://github.com/Chaitanya045/koan/issues/419): the switches in Settings don't show on and off clearly, and unselected radio buttons are hard to see in dark mode.
- [#420](https://github.com/Chaitanya045/koan/issues/420): agents can't upload files yet; a file picker waits for you.
- Folders: a pinned tab can sit above folders, and a folder tab that's closed and reopened doesn't go back into its folder.
- Protected video (Widevine) isn't signed yet, so some streaming sites may refuse to play.
- macOS asks for your keychain password once after each update, as described under **Installing**.
