## What to fix in Koan (summary for #178)

1. **Cmd+S speed:** ON is a hard jump, OFF takes ~300 ms with a 50 ms hitch and then the page snaps ~100 ms later; Zen is 120-130 ms with the page following the card each frame.
2. **Sidebar URL bar:** Zen's click opens a 440 x 270 breakout over the page with 4 top sites; Koan opens a 235 x 135 dropdown inside the sidebar with an "Ask Google" row, a "+" button and an AI chip; the URL text differs by the trailing slash.
3. **Top edge:** Koan's compact mode shows the traffic lights in a strip that pushes the page down 36 pt when the pointer touches the top; Zen shows nothing. In full screen Koan has no traffic lights at all; Zen reveals the native title strip at the screen top.
4. **Cmd+T overlay:** both 750 pt wide; Koan 8.5 pt higher, smaller magnifier (11.5 vs 13.5 pt), 11 pt vs 19 pt left padding, and an **AI Mode** chip that Zen does not have; Zen lists 4 top sites. Shift+Cmd+A does not search tabs in Koan.
